# Senior engineering evidence rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4:

- **Architecture:** technical design, tradeoffs, decomposition, migrations, or system-wide decisions.
- **Ownership:** end-to-end responsibility, production operation, judgment, and sustained outcomes.
- **Scope and scale:** supported system, traffic, data, customer, regional, or organizational boundary.
- **Technical leadership:** direction-setting, design review, standards, roadmaps, or unblocking others.
- **Cross-team influence:** alignment, stakeholder decisions, cross-functional delivery, or shared standards.
- **Mentorship:** coaching, sponsorship, onboarding, knowledge transfer, or raising team capability.
- **Operational excellence:** reliability, performance, security, cost, incidents, maintainability, or toil.
- **Business impact:** supported customer, product, revenue, cost, risk, or strategic outcome.

Score meanings:

- `0`: not demonstrated.
- `1`: weak or ambiguous mention.
- `2`: one credible example with limited scope or detail.
- `3`: clear evidence with meaningful scope or repeated examples.
- `4`: strong, specific, outcome-backed evidence at senior/lead scope.

Titles do not add points. Missing resume evidence lowers confidence about the document, not the person's actual ability.
