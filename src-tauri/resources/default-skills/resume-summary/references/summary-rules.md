# Summary rules

Prefer a compact combination of:

1. Target-relevant engineering domain.
2. Supported architecture, system, or technical depth.
3. Supported scope or measurable outcome.
4. Supported ownership, influence, or operational strength.

Use two to four differentiators rather than a technology inventory. Favor evidence-bearing nouns and verbs over `results-driven`, `innovative`, `visionary`, `world-class`, or `expert`.

Do not state `N+ years` unless structured dates make the calculation unambiguous and the user requested it. Do not infer seniority from the current title alone.
