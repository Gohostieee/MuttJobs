# Compression rules

Rank content using:

1. Target-role relevance.
2. Differentiation from other candidates and from other bullets in the resume.
3. Demonstrated seniority, architecture, ownership, influence, or operational signal.
4. Factual information value and supported outcomes.
5. Contribution to visible career progression.

Prefer removal in this order:

1. Exact or near-duplicate duties.
2. Routine responsibilities repeated in stronger recent roles.
3. Redundant technology mentions.
4. Low-information early-career detail.

Protect unique migrations, architecture decisions, unusual domain depth, measurable outcomes, and evidence that establishes progression. Do not force an experienced candidate to one page; optimize evidence density for the requested length.
