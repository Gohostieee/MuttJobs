# Evidence matching rules

Classify every material requirement:

- **Strong:** direct, specific, preferably repeated evidence at the requested depth or scope.
- **Partial:** related evidence exists, but requested depth, recency, scope, or context is not demonstrated.
- **Weak:** indirect or ambiguous evidence that should not be treated as proof.
- **None:** no supporting candidate evidence.

For each classification, quote or precisely paraphrase the requirement, cite the resume section and item, and explain the connection. Distinguish exact terminology from a defensible semantic equivalent.

Use fit bands `strong`, `competitive`, `stretch`, or `weak`. Base confidence on job-description completeness and evidence clarity. Never derive fit from raw keyword percentage.

List unsupported target terms separately. A target term is not permission to add it to candidate claims.
