# MuttJobs senior engineering rubric

Use these exact weights:

| Category | Points | High-score evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Role relevance and evidence | 18 | Direct proof for important target capabilities |
| Seniority and technical leadership | 18 | Ownership, direction, mentoring, design leadership, influence |
| Technical depth and architecture | 16 | Decisions, tradeoffs, difficult systems work, domain depth |
| Outcomes and measurable impact | 14 | Supported results and before/after evidence |
| Scope and scale | 10 | Supported system, traffic, data, user, or organizational scope |
| Operational excellence | 8 | Reliability, performance, security, cost, quality, maintainability |
| Clarity and information density | 8 | Concise accomplishment statements with little redundancy |
| ATS/readability fundamentals | 4 | Discoverable terms and parseable structure |
| Consistency and credibility | 4 | Coherent dates, tense, naming, formatting, and claims |

Total: 100.

Use bands consistently: `strong` (90-100), `competitive` (75-89), `developing` (60-74), and `needs substantial work` (0-59). Report confidence separately. The score is a diagnostic navigation aid, not a hiring prediction.
