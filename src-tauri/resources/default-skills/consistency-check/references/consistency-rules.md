# Consistency rules

Check and group:

- Present versus past tense, allowing ongoing work to remain present tense.
- Terminal punctuation and bullet voice.
- Date formats and range separators without changing the underlying dates.
- Technology and product naming such as `Node.js` versus `NodeJS`.
- Capitalization, abbreviations, and acronym expansion.
- Company/title formatting and section-heading conventions.
- Repeated phrases and duplicated bullets.
- Rich-text list and paragraph structure.

An issue exists when comparable occurrences disagree. A consistent convention is not an issue merely because another style is possible.

Safe fixes are spelling, punctuation, capitalization, or formatting changes with one clear correct result. Rewording an accomplishment or changing a date is editorial or factual, not mechanical.
